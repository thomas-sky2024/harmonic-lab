# Harmonic Lab visual system

Reference context is a compact music workstation. Chosen direction: functional studio console. Editorial and spacious directions would reduce the chord/timeline density needed for this task.

- Background #191C1D; sidebar #202425; raised surface #282D2E; border #373E3F.
- Primary text #F0F1EC; secondary #A3AEAC; tertiary #798582.
- Primary accent warm amber #E4B978; selection muted teal #89C5BB; danger #DE8880.
- Display: SF Pro rounded semibold. Body: SF Pro. Timing and notes: SF Mono.
- Spacing: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32. Radius: 6 / 10 / 14.
- Signature: piano roll with chord tones and playback cursor, connected to live keyboard.
- Three principal stages: Explore, Current Scale, Progression. Persistent transport and keyboard.
